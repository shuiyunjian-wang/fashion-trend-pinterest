Policy

This page explains how AI Fashion Trend Intelligence (the project) and TrendMuse AI (the operator) collect, use, store and delete data. It is written for transparency and for Pinterest Developer App review.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by TrendMuse AI as part of the AI Fashion Trend Intelligence project. The public site is published at https://shuiyunjian-wang.github.io/fashion-trend-pinterest/. It is an editorial, research-oriented site about women's fashion trends.

2. What this website is for

The website is used to display AI-assisted fashion trend research and content. Trend analysis on this site is produced by combining publicly available signals (such as Google Trends) with language-model (LLM) inference that helps interpret and expand those signals. All trend content is presented as editorial research, not as official data from any third party.

3. Pinterest API and Pinterest data

We may use the Pinterest API to access, retrieve and process Pinterest public data or data that Pinterest users have authorised us to access (for example, to study how fashion content performs on Pinterest, or to plan and measure our own_pins). When we do, we act in accordance with Pinterest's developer terms and only access the scopes a user or the app has explicitly granted.

  • We only request Pinterest data we need for the stated research and content-planning purpose.
  • Any authorised Pinterest data is handled as provided by Pinterest and is not represented as our own factual claim.
  • We do not use the Pinterest API to scrape or store data beyond what the authorised scopes permit.

4. Data we process

Depending on features enabled, we may process the following categories:

  • Aggregated trend signals — such as public Google Trends topics and scores, used only as research input.
  • AI-derived keywords and notes — generated by our own language models from those signals; these are clearly marked as AI-assisted, not as Google or Pinterest data.
  • Pinterest data — only public Pinterest data or data you explicitly authorise via the Pinterest API.
  • Basic technical data — such as page-view logs needed to operate the static site. We do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

5. We do not sell your personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not sell, rent or trade user personal data to advertisers or data brokers. Trend research published on the site is about broad fashion movements, not about identified individuals.

6. How we use data

  • To produce and present fashion trend research and outfit inspiration.
  • To plan, publish and measure Pinterest content for this project.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To respond to lawful requests and to enforce our terms.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. You may request deletion of personal data we hold about you, or correction of inaccurate data, by contacting us through the Contact page. We will act on verifiable requests within a reasonable period and describe what we removed. Pinterest data you authorised can be revoked at any time from your Pinterest account settings, after which we stop accessing it.

8. Separation of sources (important)

To avoid confusion, we state clearly:

  • Google Trends figures are third-party facts shown as research input; we do not edit or claim to author them.
  • LLM-derived keywords, fashion angles and notes are our own AI-assisted interpretation and are labelled as such.
  • We never present Google Trends data or LLM-derived content as official Pinterest or Google data, nor imply any endorsement by Google or Pinterest.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from users under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum in your jurisdiction).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the project evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy, about your data, or to request access or deletion, please use the project's unified Contact page. We will respond through the channel you provide there.
