Trend Report  ·  Spring Summer 2026

2026 Women's
Fashion Trends

Six directions worth building a wardrobe around this year. Each one comes with the silhouette, the colour story and a styling formula you can wear immediately — not a runway fantasy.

01

Minimalist Fashion  ·  French Style

French Minimalism

The quietest trend of 2026 and the one with the longest life. Ivory and bone knitwear, a straight-leg trouser that breaks exactly once, one structured bag and nothing else competing for attention. Effortless, but entirely engineered.

  • SilhouetteStraight, softly oversized
  • PaletteIvory, bone, espresso
  • Key pieceFine-gauge crew knit

02

Y2K  ·  Statement

Y2K Revival

Nostalgia, edited down to the pieces that still work. Low-rise denim with a longer rise than 2003, metallic sheen used as a single accent, micro shoulder bags and a tinted lens. Play it as seasoning, never as a costume.

  • SilhouetteLow-rise, cropped top
  • PaletteSilver, ice blue, charcoal
  • Key pieceMicro shoulder bag
Read the Report
03

Women's Fashion  ·  Feminine

Romantic Dresses

Movement is the whole point. Bias-cut midis, dropped waists and sheer layering in washed florals and faded rose. Ground it with a flat leather sandal or a plain boot so the softness reads intentional rather than sweet.

  • SilhouetteBias-cut, fluid midi
  • PaletteFaded rose, cream, sage
  • Key pieceSlip midi dress
Read the Report
04

Vacation Style  ·  Spring Summer 2026

Vacation Style

Resort dressing that survives a real day of walking. Matching linen sets, open-weave crochet, a shirt worn as a cover-up and one raffia bag that goes from beach to dinner. Packs flat, photographs beautifully.

  • SilhouetteRelaxed set, easy shirt
  • PaletteSand, white, sun-faded blue
  • Key pieceLinen co-ord set
Read the Report
05

Street Style  ·  Outfit Inspiration

Street Style

The real trend forecast. Sharp tailoring worn with worn-in sneakers, a leather bomber over a long skirt, denim treated as a neutral. Proportion play is the skill here — one oversized piece, everything else close to the body.

  • SilhouetteOversized top, lean bottom
  • PaletteIndigo, black, camel
  • Key pieceLeather bomber
Read the Report
06

Soft Feminine  ·  Minimalist Fashion

Soft Feminine

Femininity without frills. Rounded shoulders, ballet flats, cashmere in powder tones and a skirt that skims rather than clings. The 2026 version leans on texture and cut instead of decoration — quiet, but unmistakably feminine.

  • SilhouetteRounded, skimming
  • PalettePowder pink, oat, dove grey
  • Key pieceBallet flat
