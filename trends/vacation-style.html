Trend Report  ·  Spring Summer 2026

Vacation Style

Resort dressing that survives a real day of walking. Matching linen sets, open-weave crochet, a shirt worn as a cover-up and one raffia bag that goes from beach to dinner. Packs flat, photographs beautifully.

Resort Travel Linen Easy

01  ·  The Trend

What Vacation Style actually is

Vacation Style is the trend that has to work in real life — heat, walking, and a phone camera. The 2026 version is matching linen sets, open crochet, and a shirt you can wear as a cover-up or tied at the waist. It packs flat and photographs like a postcard.

For a label it is the highest-margin easy sell: co-ord sets, raffia, and resort pieces that ship year-round to warm markets and seasonally everywhere else. The hero is the linen co-ord.

02  ·  Why Now

The consumer signal behind it

A

Packs flat, sells far

Linen and crochet travel and re-wear; resort sells to warm markets all year.

B

Beach-to-dinner

One raffia bag that does both scenes is the most-shared detail.

C

Photographs itself

Buyers choose pieces that look good unstyled, on a phone, in sun.

05  ·  Styling Formula

A formula you can wear tomorrow

  • Build the look around one linen co-ord — it does the work.
  • Add open crochet as the texture layer, not the base.
  • Use a shirt as cover-up: open, tied, or off-shoulder.
  • One raffia bag travels beach to dinner — the only 'statement'.
  • Keep it packable. If it wrinkles to death, it is not vacation style.
