Trend Report  ·  Spring Summer 2026

Street Style

The real trend forecast. Sharp tailoring worn with worn-in sneakers, a leather bomber over a long skirt, denim treated as a neutral. Proportion play is the skill here — one oversized piece, everything else close to the body.

Sharp Layering City Tailoring

01  ·  The Trend

What Street Style actually is

Street Style is the live forecast — what actually gets worn in cities, not what walks a runway. The 2026 read is sharp tailoring against worn-in sneakers, a leather bomber over a long skirt, denim as a neutral. The skill is proportion: one oversized piece, everything else close to the body.

It is the most content-friendly trend on the list — endlessly photographable and easy to refresh weekly. For a label it is also the best cross-sell engine: one sharp piece lifts a whole casual wardrobe.

02  ·  Why Now

The consumer signal behind it

A

Proportion is the product

One oversized piece + lean base reads expensive; two oversized reads sloppy.

B

Tailoring meets sneaker

Sharp jacket with worn-in sneakers is the most-shared contrast.

C

Denim as neutral

Treat denim like a colour; it anchors every other piece.

05  ·  Styling Formula

A formula you can wear tomorrow

  • Pick one oversized piece — bomber or coat — and keep the rest lean.
  • Counter the volume with a tailored trouser or lean skirt.
  • Throw sharp tailoring over worn-in sneakers; that contrast is the look.
  • Treat denim as a neutral colour, not a category.
  • Proportion first. If two pieces are oversized, drop one.
