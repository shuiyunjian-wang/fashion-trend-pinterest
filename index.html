Editor's Note

How we read a trend

A trend only earns a page here when it passes three tests: it can be worn on an ordinary Tuesday, it works across at least two body types, and it still looks considered in twelve months' time.

That filter is why the 2026 edit is short. Six trends, each with a clear silhouette, a colour direction and a reason to exist. No filler, no borrowed hype.

  • Wearable on a normal weekday
  • Works beyond one silhouette
  • Still holds up next season
